Privacy Policy

Funky Monkeys Uploader · Effective October 1, 2026

Funky Monkeys Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Funky Monkeys Toons YouTube channel to upload and manage that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants access, the app uses:

  • The ID and name of the authorized YouTube channel, to choose where to upload.
  • Permission to upload videos and to update their title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, playlist and publishing schedule.
  • The list of the channel's own videos, to check which uploads have completed.

How information is stored and used

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computers, in a local file that is never published or shared. They are used only to make the YouTube API requests listed above for the owner's own channel. The app does not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and it does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. After access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the owner's computers. The app does not keep any other copy of YouTube data.
